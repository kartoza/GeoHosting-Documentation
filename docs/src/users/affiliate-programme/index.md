# Affiliate Programme

Resell GeoSpatialHosting products. Earn commission on every customer who comes through you. Help grow the open-source geospatial ecosystem alongside Kartoza.

The Kartoza GSH Affiliate Programme is a structured partnership for people and organisations who recommend, train on, or resell our hosted geospatial products. It exists in two tiers — **Reseller** and **Certified Trainer** — and a partner can hold both.
