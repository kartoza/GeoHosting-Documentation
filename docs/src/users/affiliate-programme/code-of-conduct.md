# Code of Conduct & Appeals

The Code of Conduct (CoC) is the written set of expectations that apply equally to every partner. Acceptance is required before your first commission accrues, and re-acceptance is required when the CoC is updated.

## The Code of Conduct

This is the short, human version. The legally-binding version is in your dashboard at <span class="ui-page-label">Profile → Code of Conduct</span> and is versioned — the version you accepted is the one that applies to you until you accept a newer version.

### Honesty in Marketing

- Describe GSH products as they are, not as marketing copy you write yourself implies them to be.
- Don't invent features. Don't claim integrations that don't exist.
- Reference Kartoza-published material (this documentation, product overview pages) rather than paraphrasing in ways that drift.
- If a customer asks you a technical question you're not sure of, say so — don't guess. Escalate to your Programme Manager.

### Customer Care

- Treat customers' data as private. Don't share customer names, projects, or usage patterns outside the partnership.
- Respond to your attributed customers within **2 business days** of an inbound question.
- Hand off cleanly when a customer needs Kartoza's help — give them a clear "this is outside what I can help with; here's how to reach Kartoza support".

### Integrity in Attribution

- Don't create referral codes for channels you don't control.
- Don't pressure customers to use your code if they intended a different partner.
- Don't fabricate signups. Synthetic signups are a permanent-suspension offence.

### Trainer-Specific

- Deliver training as advertised — content, duration, instructor.
- Issue certifications only for attendees who actually attended.
- Don't share Kartoza-supplied course content with non-attendees outside the agreed licence.

### Reputation

- Don't make public statements that mislead about Kartoza's products, financial state, or relationships.
- If you have a complaint, raise it through Programme Manager channels first. Public escalation without prior private resolution is grounds for review.

## Enforcement

The enforcement scale, in order:

| Action | Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warning** | First-time CoC concern; informational. | Visible in your dashboard. No accrual change. Stays on file for 12 months. |
| **Suspension** | Repeated warning, or a serious one-time breach. | Earnings paused. Pipeline frozen. You retain access to your dashboard and statements but no new accruals. |
| **Termination** | Severe breach, fraud, or unresolved suspension. | Programme membership ended. Prior payouts retained; future accruals forfeited. |

All enforcement decisions are made by a **Programme Manager** and recorded with a written reason in the activity log.

<br>

## Appeals

You can appeal a Warning, Suspension, or Termination. Appeals are handled by an **Appeals Reviewer** — a different Kartoza staff member to the one who issued the enforcement.

### How to Appeal

1. From the alert banner on your dashboard click <span class="ui-generic-label">Appeal</span>.
2. Provide a short statement (up to 2,000 characters) explaining why you believe the decision was wrong, and any evidence.
3. Submit.

You'll see the appeal status in the banner — **Open** while under review.

### Timeline

We commit to a decision within **30 days** of receiving the appeal — this is the published service-level agreement (SLA). Most appeals resolve in 5–10 days.

<br>

### Outcomes

| Outcome | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Upheld** | The original enforcement is removed. Any paused accruals resume. Time spent under enforcement is not refunded — but no future consequence remains. |
| **Overturned in part** | Some elements removed (e.g. suspension converted to warning). |
| **Confirmed** | The original enforcement stands. You may not re-appeal the same decision. |
| **Withdrawn** | You withdrew the appeal before review. |

The Appeals Reviewer cannot be the same person who issued the original enforcement — this is enforced by the platform.

<br>

### After the Decision

You'll receive an email with the decision, the reasoning, and (if upheld) the confirmation that your status has been restored. The decision is recorded in the activity log with a hash-chained signature for tamper-detection.

<br>

## Re-Acceptance on Updates

When the CoC is updated you'll be asked to re-accept the new version on next sign-in. Until you do, your earnings are paused — accruals continue but payouts hold. The dashboard banner explains exactly what changed between versions.

If you disagree with a CoC update you may withdraw from the programme. Prior payouts are retained; pending accruals at the time of withdrawal are paid in the next normal cycle.
