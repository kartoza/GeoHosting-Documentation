# Programme Overview

The Kartoza GSH Affiliate Programme rewards people and organisations who introduce paying customers to our hosted geospatial products — and who train them to be successful. It is a partnership: Kartoza provides product, hosting, billing, and training materials; partners provide reach, trust, and (for trainers) instructional time.

## Why We Run It

Open-source geospatial tools are powerful but the learning curve is real. Customers succeed faster when there's a local partner who:

- Speaks the customer's language (literally and idiomatically).
- Knows the local market — pricing, regulations, training expectations.
- Provides the first line of support when something feels confusing.

We can't be the right local partner for every region; we'd rather formally partner with the people who already are.

<br>

## Partner Tiers

There are two tiers. A single partner can hold one or both.

| Tier | What they do | What they earn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reseller** | Recommend / resell GSH-hosted products to organisations. Provide first-line account support. | Recurring commission on each referred subscription, for the life of the subscription, at the published rate per product. |
| **Certified Trainer** | Deliver Kartoza-certified training on GSH products. Issue certifications via the platform. Can also resell. | Per-seat training fee share + product commission on customers acquired through their training. |

The application flow is per tier — see [Applying](applying.md).

<br>

## Commissions at a Glance

- **Recurring** — paid every month for the life of the customer subscription, not a one-off finder's fee.
- **Per product** — each GSH product has its own commission rate (published in your partner dashboard).
- **Capped or uncapped** depending on tier — tier-specific terms in the [Commissions](commissions.md) page.
- **Cleared through statements** — earnings accrue daily, are summarised in a monthly statement, and paid out on a fixed cycle.

<br>

## What We Ask of Partners

- **Honesty in marketing.** Don't misrepresent the product. The [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md) is the long-form version.
- **First-line support before escalation.** When your referred customer has a question, you try to answer it first. We pick up what's beyond your scope.
- **Active recommendation, not just listing.** A partnership where you do nothing produces little revenue for either side.
- **Accept platform attribution.** Customers are matched to partners via referral codes and the 90-day click-capture cookie; we don't honour "I told them about you" claims after the fact. See [Referrals](referrals.md).

<br>

## What We Provide

- **Recurring commission**, as above.
- **A partner dashboard** that shows your pipeline, accruals, statements, and assets in one place.
- **Marketing assets** — logos, screenshots, slide templates, brochure copy — in the dashboard's Assets section.
- **Referral codes** and an embeddable widget so the customer attribution happens without you having to ask them to remember a code.
- **Programme manager contact** — a real human you can reach for help, escalation, and deal review.
- **Code of Conduct + appeals** — written-down rules that apply equally to all partners, with a defined appeals process if you disagree with an enforcement decision.
