# Partner Community

The Affiliate Programme exists alongside a partner community — somewhere you can ask other partners how they tackled an attribution question, swap training material, hear about upcoming product changes, and meet the Kartoza team behind the platform.

## Where

When Kartoza has the community space enabled, your Partner Dashboard sidebar shows a <span class="ui-page-label">Community ↗</span> link. The link opens the current community space (Discord, Slack workspace, Discourse forum, or similar — the exact tool may change over time).

If the link is absent, the community is currently disabled. Watch your monthly programme newsletter for re-launch announcements.

<br>

## What You'll Find

### Channels

- **#announcements** — Kartoza-only posting. Product launches, programme changes, upcoming maintenance windows. Read-only for partners.
- **#general** — open partner-to-partner discussion. Introductions, "anyone else tried this?" threads.
- **#training-swap** — Certified Trainers share materials, get peer review on cohort proposals, and find co-instructors for joint cohorts.
- **#commission-questions** — practical questions about accruals, attribution, statements. Programme Manager monitors.
- **#feature-requests** — what would make the platform better. Engineering reads regularly; high-signal items make it onto the roadmap.

<br>

### Office Hours

A weekly Programme Manager open call — typically 30 minutes — for any question that's too involved for a chat message. Times rotate to cover EU, Africa, and Americas business hours.

A calendar invite is posted in **#announcements** the day before each session.

<br>

### Roadmap Previews

Once per quarter, Kartoza engineering posts a short roadmap update — what's shipping in the next 90 days, what's being prototyped, what we're deprecating. Partners get this view two weeks before public release.

<br>

## Etiquette

- The community is **partners + Kartoza staff**. Not a customer support channel — don't paste customer-identifiable data.
- Be specific in questions. "Anyone seen a missing accrual?" is hard to help with; "QGIS Cloud Desktop, customer paid 2026-06-10, accrual not on my dashboard yet" is easy.
- Help when you can. Partners helping partners is what makes the community valuable — Programme Manager is a backstop, not the primary support channel.
