# Affiliate Programme Help

## FAQ

<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>How long does an application take to review?</summary>
  <div class="faq-answer">
    Reseller applications: 3–8 business days end-to-end. Trainer applications take longer because of the per-component review — allow 10–14 business days for the first decision.
    <br><br>
    If you've been waiting longer than the typical window, the application page shows the current status (In screening, Programme Manager review, More info needed). If it's "More info needed", check your email — we may be waiting on you.
  </div>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>A customer signed up via my code but isn't on my Pipeline</summary>
  <div class="faq-answer">
    The dashboard updates overnight, not in real time. Check after the nightly batch (typically before 06:00 UTC).
    <br><br>
    If they're still missing 24 h after signup, raise a Pipeline dispute from the customer's row (or, if they aren't on Pipeline at all, from the "missing attribution" link on the Pipeline page). Programme Manager reviews within 5 business days.
  </div>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>My statement is lower than I expected — why?</summary>
  <div class="faq-answer">
    Three common causes:
    <br><br>
    <strong>1. Reversals.</strong> A refund or churn during the month reverses the related accrual. Statements show reversals on a separate line.
    <br><br>
    <strong>2. FX rate.</strong> Customers billed in non-payout currencies are converted on the statement-generation date. If the rate moved between accrual and statement, the payout amount differs from the day-of-accrual estimate.
    <br><br>
    <strong>3. Subscription downgrade.</strong> A customer who downgraded mid-month earns less commission proportional to the lower invoice.
    <br><br>
    Each accrual on the statement links back to the source invoice — click through to reconcile if a specific row looks wrong.
  </div>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>I want to add a new channel after applying — can I?</summary>
  <div class="faq-answer">
    Yes. From <span class="ui-page-label">Profile → Channels</span> click <span class="ui-generic-label">Add channel</span>. The new channel goes through the same quick screening as the channels on your original application — typically 1–2 business days. Once approved you can issue referral codes scoped to it.
  </div>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>I'm a Reseller — can I add the Trainer tier later?</summary>
  <div class="faq-answer">
    Yes. From <span class="ui-page-label">Profile → Tiers</span> click <span class="ui-generic-label">Add Trainer tier</span>. You'll complete the Trainer-specific parts of the application (certification components and evidence) without redoing the Reseller parts. Per-component review applies as for first-time Trainer applicants.
  </div>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>My payout didn't arrive on the 15th</summary>
  <div class="faq-answer">
    Payouts initiate on the 15th (or next business day). Bank transfer time varies by destination:
    <br><br>
    - SEPA: 1–2 business days from initiation.
    <br>
    - Faster Payments (UK): same-day or next-day.
    <br>
    - ACH (US): 2–4 business days.
    <br>
    - International wire: 3–7 business days.
    <br><br>
    If your funds haven't arrived after the expected window, check the payout reference on the statement — it includes the bank reference your finance team can trace. If it's not landing, contact your Programme Manager.
  </div>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>I received a warning I disagree with — what do I do?</summary>
  <div class="faq-answer">
    Click <span class="ui-generic-label">Appeal</span> on the warning banner in your dashboard. Provide a short statement explaining why you believe the warning was wrong, and any evidence. An Appeals Reviewer (different person from the one who issued the warning) decides within 30 days — see <a href="../code-of-conduct/">Code of Conduct & Appeals</a>.
  </div>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>How do I leave the programme?</summary>
  <div class="faq-answer">
    From <span class="ui-page-label">Profile → Membership</span> click <span class="ui-generic-label">Withdraw from programme</span> and confirm.
    <br><br>
    Accruals up to the withdrawal date are paid on the next normal cycle. Your referral codes are deactivated immediately so no new attributions occur. Your historical statements and dashboard remain accessible for 12 months for your records.
  </div>
</details>

## Contact Your Programme Manager

Every partner has a named Programme Manager — their name and contact are on your dashboard at <span class="ui-page-label">Profile → Contact</span>.

For non-urgent questions, the [Community](community.md) is usually faster. For commercial / contractual / sensitive matters, email the Programme Manager directly.

<br>

## Log a Support Ticket

For issues that affect the platform (dashboard down, statement missing, can't sign in), log a ticket from the [Support Center](../support_center.md). Include:

- Your partner account email.
- The exact time of the issue (with timezone).
- A screenshot of the error if you have one.
- A note of anything you tried first.

Kartoza staff have read-only access to the activity log to help triage.
