# Applying to the Affiliate Programme

This page walks through the application flow. There are two tracks — **Reseller** and **Certified Trainer** — and they ask different things. You can apply to one and add the other later.

## Where to Start

Go to `https://geospatialhosting.com/#/affiliate-apply` and choose the track that fits you.

## Reseller Track

The Reseller application has four sections. Allow about 15 minutes to complete.

### 1. About You

- Legal name (individual) or registered business name (organisation).
- Country and tax jurisdiction.
- Contact email (this becomes your sign-in identity).
- Where customers know you from — website, LinkedIn, GitHub profile.

### 2. Channels

The places you'd reach customers. We accept a wide range — newsletter, podcast, YouTube channel, regional consulting practice, training cohort, community forum. For each:

- Channel name and URL.
- Approximate audience size if known.
- A one-sentence description of who the audience is and what they're interested in.

You can list as many channels as apply.

### 3. Pitch

Two short free-text fields:

- **Why partner with Kartoza?** Two or three sentences.
- **How will you introduce GSH products to your audience?** Two or three sentences — be concrete (a planned blog post, a workshop, a slot in your monthly newsletter).

### 4. Tax & Bank

- Tax / VAT number if you're invoicing as a business.
- Bank account or payment-service details for payouts.

We don't ask for proof at this stage — we'll request it during the screening step if you pass.

<br>

### Auto-Screening

When you submit, the platform auto-screens your application:

- Checks the country isn't on Kartoza's restricted-jurisdiction list.
- Cross-references your channels against any prior-terminated partner records.
- Flags obvious risk signals.

If auto-screening is clean you go straight to **Programme Manager review** (most applications). If something flags you go to **manual screening** with a checklist a screener works through.

### What Happens Next

| Status | What it means | Typical duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Submitted** | We have your application. | — |
| **In screening** | Operations team is verifying details. | 1–3 business days |
| **Programme Manager review** | Programme Manager is reading your pitch. | 2–5 business days |
| **Approved** | You're in. Welcome email + first-login link follows. | — |
| **More info needed** | We've asked clarifying questions by email. | Pauses until you reply |
| **Declined** | We're not progressing — you'll get a brief reason. You may re-apply after a stated waiting period. | — |

## Certified Trainer Track

The Trainer application adds a **per-component review** on top of the Reseller flow. We need to certify your competence to train others on specific GSH products.

### Components

You apply per **certification component** — e.g. "QGIS Cloud Desktop fundamentals", "PostGIS data modelling", "GeoServer publishing". For each component you choose:

- Your evidence: prior delivery history, recorded sessions, course materials, references.
- Whether you want **fast-track** (waives the verification interview) — only available if you have already completed Kartoza's "QGIS for trainers" course.

### Review

Each component is reviewed independently. You can be approved for some and asked for more evidence on others. Approved components let you:

- Issue Kartoza-certified completions to your students through the platform.
- Earn the per-seat training fee share on Kartoza-listed courses.
- Apply to lead **co-marketed cohorts** — cohorts the platform announces alongside its own listings (see [Training](training.md)).

<br>

## After Approval

You'll receive an email with:

- The partner-portal sign-in URL.
- A first-login link (single-use, expires in 7 days).
- A copy of the partner [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md) to read and accept before your first commission accrues.

Your first sign-in walks you through CoC acceptance and basic profile completion, then drops you on the [Partner Dashboard](dashboard.md).
