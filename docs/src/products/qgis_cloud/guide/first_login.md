# First Log In & Setting Your Password

Once your organisation has created your account and granted you access, you can register a passkey, sign in to the QGIS Cloud Desktop Portal, and launch your first desktop session.

QGIS Cloud Desktop uses passkeys for portal authentication. You do not need to create a separate portal password.

<br>

## Step 1: Register Your Passkey

Your Organisation Owner will send you an enrolment link.

1. Open the enrolment link in your web browser.
2. Choose a **Device name** for the device/authenticator you are registering.
3. Click <span class="ui-generic-label">Register passkey</span>.
4. Follow your browser's instructions to confirm the registration.

Depending on your device and available authentication methods, you may be able to use a hardware security key, your phone, or a software passkey provider such as a password manager.

Your browser will wait for the selected authenticator to respond to the passkey request. Once the authenticator confirms the request, your passkey will be registered.

<br>

<div class="image-with-caption">
  <img src="../../img/qgis-cloud-img-9.png" alt="Register Your Passkey">
</div>

<br>

## Step 2: Sign In to the Portal

After registering your passkey, open the QGIS Cloud Desktop sign-in page.

1. Enter your registered email address.
2. Enter your organisation's short identifier.
3. Click <span class="ui-generic-label">Sign in with passkey</span>.
4. Confirm the authentication request using the same authenticator you registered earlier.

After successful authentication, you will be taken to the QGIS Cloud Desktop Portal.

<br>

<div class="image-with-caption">
  <img src="../../img/qgis-cloud-img-10.png" alt="Sign In">
</div>

<br>

## Step 3: Launch a QGIS Cloud Desktop Session

On the Portal, locate the <span class="ui-page-label">Launch a QGIS session</span> section.

1. Select an available desktop image, such as **Generic**.
2. Click <span class="ui-generic-label">Launch QGIS Cloud Desktop</span>.
3. Wait while the platform provisions your desktop session.

Provisioning usually takes approximately **5-10 minutes**. You can cancel the launch from the Portal if necessary.

<br>

<div class="image-with-caption">
  <img src="../../img/qgis-cloud-img-11.png" alt="Launch Session">
</div>

<br>

Once the session status changes to `RUNNING`, the Portal displays information about your session, including the machine size, desktop image, start time, billing rate, and automatic session closure deadlines.

<br>

<div class="image-with-caption">
  <img src="../../img/qgis-cloud-img-12.png" alt="Launch Session 2">
</div>

<br>

## Step 4: Open Your Desktop

When your session is running, click <span class="ui-generic-label">Open QGIS Desktop ↗</span>.

You will see your Linux desktop running in the browser, with QGIS and the applications included in your selected desktop image.

Your home folder persists between sessions, allowing you to keep your QGIS projects, datasets, scripts, and other files.

<br>

<div class="image-with-caption">
  <img src="../../img/qgis-cloud-img-13.png" alt="Open Your Desktop">
</div>

<br>

## Step 5: Finish Your Session

When you have finished working:

1. Return to the QGIS Cloud Desktop Portal.
2. Click <span class="ui-generic-label">End session</span> to stop your desktop session.

Ending the session stops further host charges. Persistent storage charges continue while your home folder remains allocated.

<br>

<div class="image-with-caption">
  <img src="../../img/qgis-cloud-img-14.png" alt="Open Your Desktop">
</div>
